[size=4]The Mods menu, built into Nomad Drive[/size]

[i]Find, install, update and manage mods without leaving the game, and without restarting it.
A [b]Mods[/b] button on the main menu and the pause menu, made for mouse, keyboard and controller.[/i][/center]

[size=5][b][color=#f5b54e]At a glance[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Browse and subscribe[/b] to mods from this site and from Nexus Mods, all inside the game
[*][b]No restarts[/b]: mods switch on, off, update and install in the running game
[*][b]Always the newest version[/b], checked on this site [i]and[/i] on Nexus Mods
[*][b]Multiplayer that doesn't drop you[/b], with lobby badges, Match host and invite links

[size=5][b][color=#f5b54e]Find and install mods[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Browse mods[/b] from this site and press [b]Subscribe[/b] on a mod's page: it installs straight away and keeps itself updated, and unsubscribing removes it. Every file is virus-scanned on the site and checked again after download.
[*][b]Requirements come with it[/b]: the mods a mod needs are installed with it and kept updated too, and come off again once nothing you're subscribed to needs them.
[*][b]Nexus mods, installed in the game[/b]: browse Nomad Drive's Nexus Mods section as picture cards. [b]Install[/b] opens the mod's Nexus page in the menu's own browser: sign in to Nexus once, press its download button, and the menu installs it (.zip, .7z or .rar). The browser gets itself ready in the background the first time (about 150 MB), then only small updates. It stays on Nexus Mods and this site, has copy and paste with a right-click menu, and works with a controller.
[*][b]Mod packs[/b]: subscribe to a whole pack in one click, including its Nexus mods and mods added to it later. [b]Make your own packs[/b] right from the game.
[*][b]Full mod pages[/b] with the description, versions, requirements and an [b]Images[/b] tab with every picture full size.
[*][b]Only mods you can actually get[/b]: mods with no file to download, and mods that are only an .exe, are left out. [b]Refresh[/b] loads the newest mods straight away.
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#f5b54e]Keep them working[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Installed[/b]: every mod grouped by author, with search. Switch mods on and off, remove them, or [b]Update all[/b] in one go.
[*][b]Always the newest version[/b]: each mod is checked on this site [i]and[/i] on Nexus Mods, and you're offered whichever is newer. Switching over replaces the old copy, so you never end up with two. A newer copy you installed yourself (a test build, say) is never swapped for an older one.
[*][b]No restart needed[/b]: at the main menu changes happen as you click; mid-drive, press [b]Reload[/b] when you're ready, even online (mods that change multiplayer wait for the main menu). Only the Mods menu itself, preloader patchers and mods that change how the game starts still need a restart.
[*][b]Problems[/b]: when a mod fails to load, the menu says which one and why in plain words, with a one-click fix where there is one.
[*][b]Mod settings[/b]: change any mod's settings while you play, with search and reset.
[*][b]Settings page[/b]: the Mods menu's own settings (open key, language, start-up and more) in the same editor.
[*][b]Profiles[/b]: save sets of mods and switch between them in one go.
[*][b]Save backups[/b]: your saves are backed up before every change, with one-click restore.
[*][b]Export logs for mod devs[/b]: one zip on your Desktop with the BepInEx and Unity logs, every mod with its version and source, and recent errors. Your Windows user name is hidden.
[*][b]Report a bug[/b] on any mod, straight to its Bugs page.
[*][b]BepInEx is protected[/b]: its own parts can't be switched off, removed or updated from the menu, so one click can't break every mod.
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#f5b54e]Multiplayer that doesn't drop you[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Safe join[/b]: join games whose host has no mods (or not yours) and stay in. The multiplayer parts of mods the host doesn't have pause while you're in their game, an error inside a mod no longer disconnects you, and everything comes back when you leave. On by default (Mods › Multiplayer).
[*][b]Mods badge in the lobby list[/b]: [i]Mods OK[/i], [i]Mods OK · 2 optional[/i] or [i]Needs 2 mods[/i] next to each game, plus filters like [i]Only games I can join[/i] and [i]Best match first[/i].
[*][b]Check before joining[/b]: see what's different, then [b]Match host[/b] in one click on the host's exact versions. [b]Back to my mods[/b] puts your own setup back afterwards.
[*][b]When you host[/b]: mark each multiplayer mod [i]Required[/i] or [i]Optional[/i], and choose which mods' settings guests use while they're in your game. Nothing is written to their files.
[*][b]Invite links[/b]: copy a link for Discord with your mods, their versions and your join code, so friends can match your setup.
[*][b]Why I was dropped[/b]: when a game drops you, the menu says why in plain words and which mods are likely involved.
[*][b]Multiplayer check[/b]: mods that change what's sent between players are marked with a red [b]X[/b]; screen-only mods like a HUD or a cheat menu can differ.
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#f5b54e]Installation[/color][/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Download the zip and unzip it into your Nomad Drive folder (the one with Nomad Drive.exe), letting it merge.

[spoiler=For mod authors]
[size=3][b]Instant on, off and update[/b][/size]
Your mod can switch on, off and update instantly anywhere, even mid-drive, if it sets itself up when it starts and cleans up in [b]OnDestroy[/b] (unpatch Harmony, destroy what it made). Say so with one line:
[code][assembly: System.Reflection.AssemblyMetadata("NomadHotLoad", "true")][/code]
Mods without it are reloaded at the main menu, or when the player presses Reload.

[size=3][b]Nexus link[/b][/size]
Link your mod's Nexus page on its Edit page here ([b]Also on Nexus Mods[/b]) so players get your newest version from either site.

[size=3][b]Multiplayer mods[/b][/size]
Before your mod sends its own messages to the host, ask the Mods menu if it's safe, so a host without your mod never kicks the player:
[code]var t = Type.GetType("NomadModManager.NomadNet, NomadModManager");
var safe = t == null || (bool)t.GetMethod("SafeToSend").Invoke(null, new object[] { "your.plugin.guid" });[/code]
Also available: [i]Online[/i], [i]IsHost[/i], [i]HostKnown[/i], [i]HostHas(guid)[/i] and [i]PlayersWith(guid)[/i].
Does your mod only talk to other copies of itself, and keep working when other players don't have it (nobody gets dropped)? Say so, and the Mods menu won't make players match it to play together:
[code][assembly: System.Reflection.AssemblyMetadata("NomadNetOptional", "true")][/code]
[/spoiler]
